The Major League Hacking fellowship came after that. I worked on AWS Amplify's open-source tooling, triaging and resolving issues raised by people using it. Working from somebody else's bug report is a different ballgame from triaging issues you’ve encountered yourself.

I find explaining things useful when I'm learning. Writing down the steps is where I discover which part I had only understood well enough to nod along to. Some of those attempts are in the Writing window.
